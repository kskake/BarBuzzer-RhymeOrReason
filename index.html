📖 Rhyme or Reason – Game Guide

Rhyme or Reason is a quick-thinking word party game. When given a prompt word, you must respond with either a word that rhymes with it or a word that logically follows from it — before your time runs out!

How to Play

  1. Set up players and choose a target score to win.
  2. On your turn a prompt word is displayed — respond with a rhyme or a reason.
  3. Other players vote on whether your answer is acceptable.
  4. Earn a point for accepted answers; lose nothing for rejections but your dignity.
  5. First player to reach the target score wins!

Tips

  • Rhymes don't need to be perfect — near-rhymes often pass the vote.
  • Reasons should be logical or funny — the crowd decides!
  • Think fast; hesitation loses rounds.
